How It Works

From Idea To Ink In Four Steps

No mystery, no pressure. Here is exactly how a visit to Major Ink Studios goes.

01

Call Or Stop In

Ring the studio at (954) 544-2959 or come by during open hours to talk through what you want.

02

Design & Placement

We go over sizing, placement and style until the design is right. Piercings, we walk you through jewelry options.

03

Paperwork & Prep

Quick waiver, then the station is set up fresh in front of you with sterile, single-use needles.

04

Get It Done

We work at your pace, then send you out with clear aftercare so it heals the way it should.

Custom Tattoos

Ear Piercings

Lobes, helix, tragus and curated stacks, placed with a steady hand and clean technique.

  • Single or multiple placements
  • Jewelry selected in-store
  • Full aftercare instructions

Nose & Facial Piercings

Our most requested piercing. Nostril, septum and more, marked and placed to suit your face.

  • Nostril & septum
  • Symmetry checked before piercing
  • Hypoallergenic jewelry options

Body Piercings

Navel, and other body placements handled with the same care and sterile setup as everything else.

  • Belly & body placements
  • Anatomy checked first
  • Honest advice if it's not a fit

Jewelry Changes & Removals

  • Downsizing & swaps
  • Safe piercing removal
